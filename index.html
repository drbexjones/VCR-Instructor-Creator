Create a scenario-specific assignment

Enter your learning objective and clinical scenario, then generate a link students can use to complete the assignment. No account needed.

Insert your clinical scenario below. It should:

  • Present a realistic clinical situation aligned with your learning objective.
  • Include enough case information for students to investigate and justify a defensible decision (Commit).
  • Include a challenge, conflicting detail, or reasonable alternative that invites students to reconsider their decision (Complicate).
  • Leave a consequential question unanswered, whether about the case or the student's clinical knowledge (Calibrate).

A single paragraph of approximately 75–150 words is recommended. Adjust complexity to learner readiness. A challenge may also be explored through the standard Complicate question.
